How booking works

Four steps, and you know at each one what happens next.

  1. 1

    Enter your journey

    Pick-up, destination, date, passengers and bags. Your price appears before you send anything.

  2. 2

    Send your request

    Nothing is paid at this stage. You choose how you want to receive your confirmation.

  3. 3

    We confirm your chauffeur

    Elatransfer finds an available chauffeur and sends you the confirmation with the payment link.

  4. 4

    Pay, and your chauffeur sets off

    You pay your chauffeur through the link, before pick-up. Nothing is paid in the car.

Good to know before you book

Nothing to pay when you book

You pay only once your chauffeur is confirmed, through the payment link Elatransfer sends you. If no chauffeur is available, you owe nothing.

If your plans change

Before payment, you can cancel at no cost. Once paid, your chauffeur keeps the time slot for you and the ride is no longer refundable if you cancel. If the ride does not happen because of us or the chauffeur, you are refunded in full.

Who drives you

An independent professional chauffeur, under their own professional licence and insurance. Elatransfer receives your request, finds the chauffeur, confirms the ride and follows it until pick-up.

Reaching us

Online booking 24/7. Phone support from 5 am to 10 pm, Paris time. WhatsApp and email any time: contact@elatransfer.com.

Frequently asked questions

What if my flight is delayed?

Give us your flight number: we track your arrival and the chauffeur waits up to 60 minutes after the actual landing, at no charge.

How do I pay?

Directly to your chauffeur, through the payment link Elatransfer sends you with the confirmation. Your chauffeur sets off as soon as payment is received. Nothing is paid when you book.

Where will the chauffeur meet me?

At the pick-up address you gave. At an airport or station, you can choose a meet-and-greet with a sign bearing your name.

Hotel, agency or business?

Elatransfer also creates a booking page in your name, a QR code for your guests and a space for your front desk, then handles every ride.
