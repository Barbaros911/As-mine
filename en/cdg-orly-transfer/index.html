Your connection, terminal to terminal

Charles de Gaulle is north-east of Paris, Orly is to the south. A private transfer links the two directly, whatever the time of your flights.

Both terminals, chosen by you

You pick your arrival terminal and your departure terminal in the booking form: your chauffeur meets you at one and drops you at the other.

Your first flight is followed

Give the number of the flight you arrive on: the pick-up time adjusts to its actual landing, and your chauffeur waits up to 60 minutes after it.

Keep a margin for your connection

The journey time between the two airports depends on traffic. Allow enough time for check-in and security: no one can guarantee a connection.

Travelling with others or many bags

A van carries up to 7 passengers. Enter your number of bags so that the right vehicle comes.

How booking works

Four steps, and you know at each one what happens next.

  1. 1

    Enter your journey

    Pick-up, destination, date, passengers and bags. Your price appears before you send anything.

  2. 2

    You pay your chauffeur through the link, before pick-up. Nothing is paid in the car.

Good to know before you book

Nothing to pay when you book

You pay only once your chauffeur is confirmed, through the payment link Elatransfer sends you. If no chauffeur is available, you owe nothing.

If your plans change

Before payment, you can cancel at no cost. Once paid, your chauffeur keeps the time slot for you and the ride is no longer refundable if you cancel. If the ride does not happen because of us or the chauffeur, you are refunded in full.

Who drives you

An independent professional chauffeur, under their own professional licence and insurance. Elatransfer, a booking and dispatch service, receives your request, finds the chauffeur in a wide network of professionals, confirms the ride and follows it until pick-⁠up.

Reaching us

Online booking 24/7. Live support by phone and WhatsApp from 7 am to 10 pm, Paris time. You can write on WhatsApp or by email at any time, and we reply from 7 am: contact@elatransfer.com.

Frequently asked questions

What if my flight is delayed?

Give us your flight number: we track your arrival and the chauffeur waits up to 60 minutes after the actual landing, at no charge.

How do I pay?

Directly to your chauffeur, through the payment link Elatransfer sends you with the confirmation. Your chauffeur sets off as soon as payment is received. Nothing is paid when you book.

Where will the chauffeur meet me?

At the pick-up address you gave. At an airport or a main railway station, your chauffeur waits at the arrivals exit or as your train arrives, with a sign bearing your name: this meet-and-greet is included in the price shown.
